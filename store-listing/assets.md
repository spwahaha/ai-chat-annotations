# Store asset upload map

Upload the files in this order.

## Required

- Store icon, 128×128: `assets/store/store-icon-128.png`
- Small promotional tile, 440×280: `assets/store/promo-small.png`
- Screenshot 1, 1280×800: `assets/store/screenshots/01-add-to-question.png`
- Screenshot 2, 1280×800: `assets/store/screenshots/02-annotate-reference.png`
- Screenshot 3, 1280×800: `assets/store/screenshots/03-multiple-references.png`
- Screenshot 4, 1280×800: `assets/store/screenshots/04-chatgpt-references.png`

## Optional

- Marquee promotional tile, 1400×560: `assets/store/promo-marquee.png`

The original uncropped screenshots are kept under `assets/store/raw` locally and are intentionally excluded from Git.
